# AeroLearn 2.2 — Compact Academic Workspace

This pass makes the most frequently used academic pages visibly more compact, colorful and organized.

## Major redesigns
- Assessment Management rebuilt as a connected workspace.
- Submit Assessment Scores rebuilt with compact 3-step selection, live score-entry metrics and dense colored score table.
- Assessment Results rebuilt with pass/fail statistics, class average and readable result badges.
- Attendance spacing, controls, summary cards and tables compressed for better information density.
- Added a reusable `workspace.css` visual system for future data-heavy pages.

## Design direction
- Smaller headers and controls rather than oversized cards.
- Dark academic-blue table headers.
- Alternating soft row colors and clear hover states.
- Color-coded pills for categories, pending/completed states, and pass/fail.
- Compact metric strips instead of large dashboard tiles.
- Responsive layouts preserve usability on tablet and mobile.
