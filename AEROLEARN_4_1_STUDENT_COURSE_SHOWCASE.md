# AeroLearn 4.1 — Student Course Showcase

## Student course visual refresh
- Course cards no longer crop uploaded course artwork. They use a full-image `object-fit: contain` treatment over a softly blurred version of the same artwork.
- Course cards are capped to a professional readable width instead of stretching one course across the entire page.
- The course workspace hero was rebuilt as a split identity panel: course information on the left and the complete course artwork on the right.
- The long course description was removed from the hero so it is not duplicated.
- The full description now appears once under **About this course**.
- The overview was rebuilt with a compact briefing panel and modern metric cards.
- Student-facing statistics now emphasize materials, assessments, attendance sessions and credit hours instead of exposing the roster count.
- Responsive behavior was added for tablets and phones.

No server/database changes are required for this visual pass.
