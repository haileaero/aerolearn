# AeroLearn v6.2 — Attendance Year V + Delete Sessions

- Attendance Study Year selectors now always provide Year I, Year II, Year III, Year IV and Year V.
- Saved Attendance actions are simplified to Edit, Print and Delete.
- Delete uses the shared AeroLearn confirmation dialog and immediately removes the deleted row from the saved-session results.
- Deleting a session frees that course/date/period combination so attendance can be registered again.
- Backend deletion authorization prevents instructors from deleting attendance outside their assigned courses; Admin retains management access.
- Footer version updated to v6.2.
